GLOBAL NUMBER FORMAT TEST
=========================

This file proves Veri-Fact reads numbers the way the whole world writes them,
not just the US/UK way. Every line below states the SAME correct figure in a
different convention, so all of them should come back GREEN.

European (dot groups, comma decimal)
    Global revenue in Q3 2024 was 4.800.000,00 and churn was 2,3%.

Swiss (apostrophe groups)
    Global revenue in Q3 2024 was 4'800'000.00 and churn was 2.3%.

US / UK (comma groups, dot decimal)
    Global revenue in Q3 2024 was 4,800,000.00 and churn was 2.3%.

Space separated (SI / French style)
    Global revenue in Q3 2024 was 4 800 000,00 and churn was 2,3%.

Scale suffixes work with any convention
    Global revenue in Q3 2024 was 4,8 Mio and total sales were 5,25M.
    Global revenue in Q3 2024 was 4.8M and total sales were 5.25M.
